FAQ

Questions from schools

Do families have to pay upfront for orders?

Families pay when they order through the store. The school does not buy or stock inventory.

Can we run a fundraiser through the store?

Yes. We can set up fundraising options for PTOs, booster clubs and teams. Tell us your goals in the form and we will walk you through the options.

Can we open the store only for a certain window?

Yes. Pre-order windows are popular for homecoming, graduation and season-opening games. Outside those windows, the store can stay open for restocks or close entirely.

What artwork do you need?

A PNG logo with a transparent background works best, plus your school colors. If you have approved mascot or spirit art, send that too and we will confirm print quality before launch.

Can we ship to the school instead of to homes?

Yes. You can choose home delivery, school office pickup, or a combination, depending on how your families prefer to receive orders.

Do you work with districts with several schools?

Yes. Districts can run one parent store with a collection for each school, all managed from a single account.
