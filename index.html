FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to get started?

Just a logo as a PNG file with a transparent background if possible, and a rough idea of what you want to offer. We will help with the rest.

Do I pay for inventory up front?

No. Products are produced when customers order, so there is no inventory to buy, store or write off.

Can we keep the store private to our employees?

Yes. Stores can be invite-only, limited to a company email domain, or open to the public. Access is set up with you during launch.

Who handles shipping and returns?

We do. Orders ship directly to the buyer, and we handle returns and replacements for defective items.

Can we add new products later?

Absolutely. You can ask us to add items or new drops at any time, and seasonal or limited collections are easy to launch.
